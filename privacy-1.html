⚡ Jolt Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Jolt is a social app where people share posts, stories and messages. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your account details: email, username, name and profile photo.
  • What you create: posts, photos, videos, stories, comments, reactions, polls, notes and hashtags.
  • Your messages, group chats and voice notes.
  • Basic device and usage data, such as when you open the app, to keep it working and fast.

How we use it

  • To run Jolt: sign you in, show your posts, deliver your messages and send alerts.
  • To keep the app safe and fix problems.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase, a service that keeps app data on secure servers. Jolt is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services have their own privacy policies.

Who can see your content

Posts and stories can be seen by other Jolt users, based on your privacy settings in the app. Private messages are only visible to the people in that chat.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: open Me, then Settings, then Delete account. This removes your profile, posts and messages from Jolt.

If you cannot open the app, email us at the address below and we will delete your account.

Children

Jolt is not for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
